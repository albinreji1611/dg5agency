WHY UAE BRANDS PICK DG5

Live engagements, not borrowed credentials.

A lot of "UAE digital marketing agencies" are a sales rep and a WhatsApp group backed by a delivery team overseas that's never seen a Dubai storefront. DG5 runs UAE accounts the same way we run Bangalore ones — one accountable team, reachable on your working hours, that will tell you the honest number before it tells you the pitch.

What actually differs about marketing in the UAE

Search behaviour splits English/Arabic, Ramadan and peak-season timing reshapes every media plan, and a huge share of D2C traffic is mobile-first on Instagram and TikTok rather than desktop search. We plan around that from day one, not as an afterthought.

  • Bilingual creative, not translated creative — Arabic copy written for the market, not run through a translator after the English version ships.
  • UAE-hours reporting — a call slot and a Slack channel that line up with your day, not a nine-hour lag to Bangalore.
  • AED invoicing, transparent scope — no dollar conversion surprises and no line items you didn't agree to upfront.
  • One team across markets — the same web dev, SEO and ads specialists who built our Bangalore playbook, not a separate junior "international" desk.

Who this is for.

D2C and retail brands selling into Dubai and Abu Dhabi, UAE-based SaaS and services companies selling regionally, and India- or Europe-headquartered brands opening a GCC storefront for the first time and needing a team that already understands both ends.

Who this isn't for.

Brands looking for the cheapest possible retainer with no reporting. We scope UAE engagements the same way we scope every DG5 engagement — against a number, with a monthly review attached to it.
