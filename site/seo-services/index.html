WHAT SEO ACTUALLY MEANS HERE

Rankings are the receipt, not the goal.

SEO is the discipline of making a site easy for search engines to crawl, easy for a reader to trust, and easy for an algorithm to match against real intent. Done well, it is compounding: a page you build and fix once keeps earning traffic for years, at a falling cost per visitor. Done badly, it is a treadmill of one-off tricks that stop working the moment an algorithm update lands.

Search itself has also split in two. People still type into Google, but a growing share now ask ChatGPT, Gemini, or Perplexity for a recommendation instead of scrolling ten blue links. DG5's SEO work covers both: the technical and content foundation that ranking depends on, plus the structured, citable content that AI answer engines pull from when they summarise a category for a searcher.

What we actually do differently

Most SEO pitches promise "page one" and leave the definition of success vague until the invoice arrives. We scope every SEO engagement against a specific, agreed number — organic sessions, qualified leads, or revenue — and report against that number monthly, in plain language, not a wall of unexplained metrics.

  • Technical audit first — a full crawl and Core Web Vitals check before any content work starts.
  • Intent-first keyword research — tied to what a buyer is actually trying to do, not just search volume.
  • Links earned, never bought — outreach and PR, not a bulk link package that risks a penalty later.
  • AI-answer-engine ready — schema and structured data so tools like ChatGPT can cite you accurately.

Who this is for.

Ecommerce and retail brands competing on category and product pages, SaaS and B2B companies where one organic lead is worth months of ad spend, and local service businesses that live or die by their Google Business Profile.

Who this isn't for.

Brands wanting a page-one guarantee with no reporting. We scope SEO engagements the same way we scope every DG5 engagement — against a number, with a monthly review attached to it.
